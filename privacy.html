Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Carolina Reaper Initiative ("CRI") is a desktop creative workflow and media publishing application.

Information Used by CRI

CRI may access information provided by third-party platforms when a user explicitly authorizes CRI to connect to that platform. This may include basic account information, account identifiers, authorization credentials, and information necessary to perform requested publishing operations.

Media and Publishing

CRI processes media selected by the user, including audio, images, and video. When the user requests a publishing or upload operation, the selected content and information required for that operation may be transmitted to the selected third-party platform.

Authentication

CRI uses authorization mechanisms provided by supported third-party services. Authentication credentials may be stored locally on the user's device where necessary to maintain an authorized connection. CRI does not require users to provide their social-media passwords directly to the application.

Third-Party Services

CRI may interact with services such as TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube when authorized by the user. Information transmitted to those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

CRI does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to third-party services only as necessary to perform functionality requested or authorized by the user.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke CRI's access to a connected platform through the account or application-management controls provided by that platform. Locally stored CRI authorization information may also be removed from the user's device.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CRI's functionality changes. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be submitted through the Carolina Reaper Initiative project repository.
